html2epub

Save the article on any web page as an EPUB, e.g. for your reMarkable.

Install

Drag this button to your bookmarks bar:

Save as EPUB

Or create a bookmark by hand and paste this as its URL:

Use

  1. Open an article.
  2. Click Save as EPUB in your bookmarks.
  3. The EPUB downloads. Images are embedded when the site allows them to be downloaded.

Limitations

  • Some sites block scripts from other sites (Content-Security-Policy). The bookmarklet can't run there.
  • Images the browser isn't allowed to fetch (cross-origin without CORS) are left out.

Everything runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Source: github.com/theboywho/html2epub.
